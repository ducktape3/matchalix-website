About

Flavours you don't see every day

Matcha Lix mixes matcha, coffee and chocolate with fruit, caramel and cream. Blueberry under matcha. Strawberry foam on chocolate. Banana where you didn't expect it.

Layered so you can see it

Every drink is poured in layers over ice, so you know exactly what you're about to sip. They look as good as they taste, and yes, you can take a photo first.
